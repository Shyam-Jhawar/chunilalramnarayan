Since 1885 · Four generations

Midnapore's oldest saree house

More than 140 years ago, two brothers — Shri Chunilal ji Jhawar and Shri Ramnarayan ji Jhawar — opened a small shop in Midnapore on one promise: honest prices, genuine quality, and respect for everyone who walked through the door. That shop grew into Chunilal Ramnarayan, today the oldest store in Midnapore town.

The legacy passed to Shri Chunilal ji's son, Shri Premsukh ji Jhawar, and then to the third generation — Shri Liladhar ji Jhawar, Shri Radhakishan ji Jhawar and Shri Chand Ratan ji Jhawar — who built it into the name families across Midnapore turn to for every celebration. Today they guide the shop alongside the fourth generation of the Jhawar family.

Four generations on, the promise hasn't changed: honest prices, genuine quality, and the kind of personal attention that turns customers into family. Come and experience the trust that generations of Midnapore families have relied on.
